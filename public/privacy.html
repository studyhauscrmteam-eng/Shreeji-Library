Privacy Policy

SHREEJI READING LIBRARY · BHAVNAGAR

Last updated: October 2026. ShreeJi Reading Library ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Booking enquiries: your name, phone number, email address (optional), selected membership plan and message when you submit the seat booking form.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information collected automatically to keep the website secure and working.
  • Cookies: a small preference cookie remembering your cookie-banner choice and language selection.

2. How we use your information

  • To contact you about your seat booking enquiry via phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To manage memberships, seat allocation and library operations.
  • To improve our website and services.

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data sharing

Your enquiry details are stored securely and are visible only to authorised library staff for admission processing. We share data with law enforcement only when legally required.

4. Data retention

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed for admission and operational purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.
